Original fictional silver executive sedan developed with AI-assisted tools.
Latest reference-rebuild snapshot, 4 November 2026. Includes editable Blender
5.2 source, a combined FBX, and 22 separate FBX groups with documented pivots.
486 mesh objects, 701,004 triangles, 36 materials.
Centimetre units (scale 0.01). No skeleton or animation is included.
Separate groups include body, glass, four doors and door windows, wheels,
calipers, steering wheel, gear lever and two gauge needles. Door/cockpit pivots
are prepared for manual setup; users must implement their own articulation.
Separate-FBX/manifest.json describes the export and material values.

Materials are procedural/parameter based. Box-projected UVs are supplied;
there is no production texture atlas. Blender node shading may need rebuilding
in other tools. Glass needs translucency setup. No image textures are required.

This is a high-detail development preview. Bumper/shoulder surfaces, trim,
optical lamp details and cabin fidelity remain in development. Some body
triangles can produce near-zero-tangent warnings in Unreal. No automatic LODs,
collision setup, damage rig, vehicle physics, Unreal project, sounds, code or
character are included. Not production low-poly, an engineering model,
watertight 3D-printing geometry or an exact reconstruction of a real vehicle.
Gallery renders show this delivered snapshot. See technical-details.json.
